Why this is hard anywhere else

Offensive tooling writes to one log; defensive telemetry to another. Two systems, two clocks, and each side's record is the one the other side has reason to doubt. Reconstructing "was that us?" afterwards is manual, slow, and contested.

Here both are the same log, and the log can prove it was not edited — see Integrity Proof. That does not make a verdict here proof of causation: same target inside a time window is grounds for attributable, not caused by. "Not ours" is only claimed when a complete record of our own activity exists for that window; with no record at all, this reports inconclusive rather than innocent.
