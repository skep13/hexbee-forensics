If you're completely new

HexBee collects digital evidence and keeps it in a log that can't be quietly edited. Three ideas cover most of it:

  • Event — one thing that was observed. A USB stick plugged in, a suspicious file found, someone logging in.
  • Incident — related events grouped together, so you see a sequence instead of a pile. HexBee does this for you.
  • Case — the folder for one job. Make one before you start; everything you find gets filed against it, and the report comes out of it at the end.

Every record is sealed with a fingerprint of the one before it. That's what lets you show, later, that nothing was altered — and it's the difference between notes and evidence.
